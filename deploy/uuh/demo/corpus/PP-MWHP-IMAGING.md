# Mountain West Health Plan — Medical Policy MWHP-IMG-004

**SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL PAYER POLICY**

**Subject:** Advanced Imaging of the Lumbar Spine
**Effective:** 2026-01-01 · **Supersedes:** MWHP-IMG-003 (2024-06-01)

## 1. Scope

Governs medical-necessity determination for MRI and CT of the lumbar spine,
including CPT 72148 (MRI lumbar spine without contrast), 72149 (with contrast),
and 72158 (without and with contrast).

## 2. Coverage criteria

Advanced imaging of the lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when
the clinical record documents **either** of the following:

**(a) Failed conservative therapy.** A minimum of **six weeks** of documented
conservative management without clinically meaningful improvement. Qualifying
conservative management comprises supervised physical therapy, anti-inflammatory
pharmacotherapy, or both. The record must state duration and response.

**— OR —**

**(b) Progressive neurological deficit.** Objective neurological findings on
physical examination that are new or progressive, including but not limited to
motor weakness, foot drop, demonstrable sensory loss in a dermatomal
distribution, or reflex asymmetry. Documented weakness graded below 5/5 in a
myotomal distribution satisfies this criterion.

Criteria (a) and (b) are **independent and alternative**. Satisfaction of either
is sufficient. Simultaneous satisfaction of both is not required and shall not
be construed as necessary.

## 3. Expedited review

Requests documenting progressive motor deficit under 2(b) are eligible for
expedited review within 72 hours of receipt.

## 4. Exclusions

Advanced imaging is not covered for uncomplicated acute low back pain of fewer
than six weeks' duration absent findings under 2(b), or for screening in the
asymptomatic patient.

## 5. Documentation

Determinations are made on the documentation submitted with the authorisation
request. Where relevant clinical findings exist in the record but were not
submitted, the appropriate remedy is appeal with the complete record attached.
