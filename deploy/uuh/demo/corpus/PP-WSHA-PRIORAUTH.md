# Wasatch State Health Alliance — Provider Manual, Section 7

**SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL PAYER POLICY**

**Subject:** Prior Authorization Requirements and Appeals
**Revision:** 2026-02 · Applies to all commercial and exchange products

## 7.1 Services requiring prior authorization

Prior authorization must be obtained **before** the service is rendered for:

- Advanced diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT, PET), all sites
- Sleep studies, attended and unattended
- Interventional pain procedures, including facet joint injections and medial
  branch blocks
- Bariatric surgery and associated pre-surgical consultation
- Physical therapy beyond the twelfth visit in a benefit year
- Non-emergent inpatient admission

Authorization obtained after the service is rendered is not honoured
retroactively except in documented emergency circumstances.

## 7.2 Requesting authorization

Submit through the Wasatch provider portal with supporting clinical
documentation. Standard determinations are issued within 14 calendar days;
urgent requests within 72 hours.

## 7.3 Appeals

An adverse determination may be appealed within **180 days of the date of the
adverse determination notice**. Wasatch State Health Alliance affords a
180-day appeal window; providers are cautioned that this period differs from
that of other payers and should not be assumed to apply to non-Wasatch claims.

Required with an appeal:

- Completed appeal cover sheet (WSHA-7A)
- Clinical records relevant to the service in dispute
- The adverse determination notice

## 7.4 Peer-to-peer review

A treating provider may request peer-to-peer discussion with a Wasatch medical
director within 14 days of an adverse determination. Peer-to-peer review does
not extend the 180-day appeal window.
