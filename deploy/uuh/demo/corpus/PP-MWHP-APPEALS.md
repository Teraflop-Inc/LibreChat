# Mountain West Health Plan — Administrative Policy MWHP-APL-001

**SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL PAYER POLICY**

**Subject:** Provider Appeals — Medical Necessity Determinations
**Effective:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Filing deadline

An appeal of a medical-necessity denial must be received by Mountain West
Health Plan within **90 days of the remittance advice date**. The remittance
advice date, not the date of service and not the date of receipt by the
provider, starts the period.

Appeals received after 90 days will not be considered on the merits.

## 2. Required documentation

A complete appeal comprises **all four** of the following. Submissions missing
any item are returned as incomplete and do not toll the 90-day period.

1. **Signed appeal request form** (MWHP-F-220), signed by an authorised
   representative of the submitting provider.
2. **Clinical documentation** supporting medical necessity, including the
   relevant encounter notes and examination findings for the date of service.
3. **Attestation from the ordering physician**, signed and dated, stating the
   clinical basis on which the service was ordered.
4. **Copy of the remittance advice** showing the denied line item.

## 3. Submission

Appeals may be submitted through the provider portal or by mail to the Appeals
Unit. Portal submission is recommended; a confirmation number is issued on
receipt and constitutes proof of timely filing.

## 4. Determination timeline

Standard appeals are determined within 30 calendar days of receipt of a
complete submission. Expedited appeals, where delay would jeopardise health,
are determined within 72 hours.

## 5. Second-level review

A provider dissatisfied with a first-level determination may request
second-level review within 60 days of the first-level decision.
